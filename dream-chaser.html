Dream Chaser: an AI job search assistant

mosAI hackathon by Product Folks · October 2025 · Product MVP

Problem: Job seekers, especially those looking for roles abroad, juggle many job boards, rewrite their resume for every role, struggle to find employers that sponsor visas, and lose track of where they applied.

What we built: one MVP that brings job search, AI resume optimisation, AI-assisted applications and application tracking into a single dashboard.

Why it matters: it turns a scattered, manual process into one guided journey, so candidates spend their energy on the right roles instead of on admin.

The problem

Searching for a job today means switching between job boards, company sites and social media; tailoring a resume to pass applicant tracking systems for each role; and keeping track of applications, emails and interviews by hand. For international candidates there is one more hurdle: finding out which employers actually sponsor visas or support relocation, information that is rarely easy to find.

What we built

Smart job searchSearch across sources with filters for experience, salary and job type, plus visa sponsorship detection.
AI resume builderTailors a resume to a specific job description and shows an ATS compatibility score with tips to improve it.
Intelligent applicationsApplies to matching roles based on preferences, with cover letters personalised from the candidate's own story.
Application analyticsA dashboard of applications, interviews, offers and response rates, with AI insights on what is working.
Email trackingMonitors a dedicated job search inbox to update application status automatically.
Global opportunitiesHighlights roles with relocation support and visa sponsorship for candidates moving countries.

Key product decisions

  • Visa sponsorship as a first-class filter, not an afterthought, because for international candidates it decides whether a role is worth applying to at all.
  • Quality over volume in automated applications: a daily application limit and preference settings, so automation targets the right roles rather than flooding employers.
  • Personal stories in cover letters: the candidate writes their own story once, and the AI personalises from it, keeping a human voice in every application.
  • A dedicated job search inbox: recommended so tracking stays organised and separate from personal email.
  • One dashboard for the whole journey, so progress is visible in one place instead of scattered across tools.

Responsible AI: what I would strengthen next

  • Credentials: the MVP's email setup asks for a password. A production version should rely only on secure OAuth sign-in and never store passwords.
  • Automated applications: keep a human review step before anything is sent, respect job boards' terms of use, and make the daily limits a safeguard rather than a growth lever.
  • Honest resumes: optimisation should rephrase and highlight real experience, never invent skills to beat an ATS.
  • Transparency: explain how the ATS score and recommendations are calculated, so candidates can trust and question them.
  • Personal data: resumes and inboxes hold sensitive information, so minimise what is stored and give users clear control to delete it.
